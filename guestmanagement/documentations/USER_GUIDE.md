# Guest Management System - User Guide

## Overview

The Guest Management System (GMS) is a web-based application designed to streamline the process of managing guests, invitations, and visits for organizations. This guide is intended for end-users such as receptionists, administrative staff, and event coordinators who will be using the system on a daily basis.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the System

To access the Guest Management System:
1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the application URL (provided by your system administrator)
3. Log in using your credentials (email and password)

### User Roles

The system supports three primary user roles:

1. **Administrator (ADMIN)**: Full access to all system features including user management
2. **Employee (EMPLOYEE)**: Standard user with basic guest management capabilities
3. **Receptionist (RECEPTIONIST)**: Focused on check-in/check-out operations and visit management

Your role determines what actions you can perform within the system.

## Core Functionalities

### Managing Guests

Guests represent individuals who will be visiting your organization.

#### Creating a New Guest
1. Navigate to the "Guests" section from the main menu
2. Click the "Add New Guest" button
3. Fill in the required information:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address (must be unique)
   - Phone Number (must be unique)
   - Identification Details (optional):
     - Identification Type (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License)
     - Identification Number
4. Click "Save" to create the guest record

#### Viewing and Searching Guests
- Use the guest list to browse all registered guests
- Apply filters to narrow down results by name, email, or other criteria
- Click on any guest to view their detailed information and history

#### Editing Guest Information
1. Locate the guest you wish to edit
2. Click the "Edit" button on their record
3. Modify the necessary fields
4. Save your changes

### Managing Invitations

Invitations are requests for guests to visit your organization for a specific purpose and time period.

#### Creating an Invitation
1. Navigate to the "Invitations" section
2. Click "Create New Invitation"
3. Select or search for the guest (must exist in the system)
4. Enter invitation details:
   - Purpose of visit (detailed description)
   - Expected arrival date and time
   - Expected departure date and time (optional)
   - Select the inviting employee (usually pre-filled with your account)
5. Click "Save" to create the invitation

#### Invitation Lifecycle
Invitations progress through these stages:
1. **Pending**: Initial state after creation
2. **Approved**: Authorized by staff/admin (not the creator)
3. **Rejected**: Declined by staff/admin (with reason provided)
4. **Cancelled**: Voided by creator or system

#### Managing Invitations
- View all invitations in the invitations list
- Filter by status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled)
- Take action on pending invitations:
  - **Approve**: Authorize the visit (staff/admin only, cannot approve your own)
  - **Reject**: Decline with reason (staff/admin only, cannot reject your own)
  - **Cancel**: Void the invitation (creator can cancel Pending/Approved invitations)

### Managing Visits

Visits represent actual instances when guests check-in and check-out of your organization.

#### Check-in Process
When a guest arrives for their approved visit:
1. Navigate to the "Visits" section
2. Click "Check-in Guest"
3. Select the approved invitation
4. The system automatically records the check-in time
5. The visit status becomes "Checked In"

#### Check-out Process
When a guest is ready to leave:
1. Navigate to the "Active Visits" or search for the guest's visit
2. Locate the visit in "Checked In" status
3. Click "Check-out" on the visit record
4. The system records the check-out time
5. The visit status becomes "Checked Out"

#### Visit Management
- View visit history for any guest
- Monitor currently checked-in guests
- Generate reports on visit durations and frequencies

## Common Tasks and Workflows

### Processing a Visitor Arrival
1. Verify the guest has an approved invitation
2. Upon arrival, initiate check-in through the Visits section
3. Confirm guest identity matches invitation details
4. Complete check-in process
5. Escort guest to their destination

### Processing Visitor Departure
1. When guest indicates they're leaving, locate their active visit
2. Initiate check-out process
3. Confirm all belongings are collected
4. Complete check-out
5. Guest exits the premises

### Handling Special Situations
- **Expired Invitations**: Invitations cannot be used after expected departure time
- **Changes to Plans**: Update invitation details while in Pending status
- **Early/Late Arrivals**: System records actual times regardless of expected times
- **No-shows**: Invitations remain in system; follow up with guest as needed

## Best Practices

### Data Accuracy
- Always verify guest identification when creating records
- Ensure contact information is current and correct
- Double-check invitation details before approval

### Security
- Never share your login credentials
- Log out when leaving your workstation unattended
- Report any suspicious activity to system administrators

### Efficiency
- Use search and filter functions to locate records quickly
- Pre-create invitations for expected visitors
- Regularly review and clean up old records as per organizational policy

## Getting Help

If you encounter issues or have questions:
1. Consult your supervisor or team lead
2. Refer to the system administrator for technical issues
3. Check for updated documentation on the internal knowledge base
4. Provide feedback through official channels to improve the system

## Appendix: Glossary of Terms

- **Guest**: An individual visiting the organization
- **Invitation**: Authorization for a guest to visit during a specific time period
- **Visit**: The actual occurrence of a guest checking in and out
- **Pending**: Invitation awaiting approval
- **Approved**: Invitation authorized for use
- **Rejected**: Invitation declined with reason
- **Cancelled**: Invitation voided by creator
- **Checked In**: Guest has arrived and completed check-in
- **Checked Out**: Guest has departed and completed check-out
